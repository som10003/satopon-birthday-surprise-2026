Anne Shirley's Liniment Cake

🌸

Prep: 25 min | Cook: 25-30 min | Yield: 10 servings

Cake: Flour, baking powder, butter, sugar, eggs, milk, vanilla, raspberry jam

Frosting: Butter, raspberry jam, powdered sugar, fresh raspberries

Preheat to 350°F. Mix dry ingredients. Cream butter & sugar. Fold in flour, milk, vanilla, eggs. Bake 25-30 min. Cool. Make frosting. Layer cake with jam & frosting. Garnish with fresh raspberries.
